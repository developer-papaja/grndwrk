As someone exploring a product idea, I want abstract thoughts high, topics side by side and recent changes in front,
so that I can read the shape of the idea at a glance.

## Acceptance
- Changing a node's altitude moves its card up or down to that altitude's guide line
- Each topic is its own column, and nodes without a topic sit in a "no topic" column at the right
- The most recently changed nodes sit in front; untouched ones sit further back and dimmer
- Links across topics are drawn faint and never pull two topic columns together
- Nodes are word cards with the title, and altitude guides show words, not numbers
