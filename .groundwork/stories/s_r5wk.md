As someone moving around the map, I want orbiting, panning and clicking to just work,
so that navigation feels easy and never deep.

## Acceptance
- Holding space and dragging pans the map
- A click that moves a few pixels still counts as a click, on the card or its words
- `F` fits the whole map in view
- Tools in the bottom bar are named in words or tooltips, not in a line of key codes
