As someone developing a thought, I want it to open in a panel beside the map,
so that I can write about it without losing my place.

## Acceptance
- Clicking a card opens it in a docked panel on the right; nothing navigates away
- The full title shows as text and becomes editable when clicked
- Pasting or dropping an image saves it in `.grndwrk/files/<node id>/` and links it from the notes
- The panel has Minimize and Close at the top
