As someone exploring a product idea, I want to type a line and press Enter to add a thought,
so that capture never slows my thinking down.

## Acceptance
- Typing a title in the bottom bar and pressing Enter writes a new node file with only a title required
- `?` makes a question and `!` makes a decision, with or without a space after
- The new card lights up for a few seconds and a note says where it went
- Adding a title that already exists adds nothing and points at the existing card
