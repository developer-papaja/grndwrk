Taken from the answer: the protocol is the only fixed part. Everything else is the team's own: where it runs, which database, how people log in, who may see and change what, how the page looks and which views it has. The kits are examples to start from and change, not products grndwrk maintains, and `grndwrk store check` is what says a team's own version works. grndwrk's side knows only an address, a token and the protocol.
