Built into the protocol: a third kind of token next to read and write, **comment**. A phone or a staging page logs in the way the team's store does and gets one; it may only leave a comment as `inbox/<commenter>/<id>.json` (under its own name, so grndwrk knows who it was from the path, never from what the device wrote) and read `status/<commenter>.json`, nothing of the map. Any teammate's grndwrk that syncs files the comment as one from the Wi-Fi or the relay (marked when suspicious, words quoted) and keeps the status up to date. The relay stays for teams without a store. The Comment button and the iOS package learn to send there in a later step, once the sync is in.

Started 2026-11-16 on the branch `shared-store`: the protocol (`docs/store-protocol.md`), `grndwrk store check`, adapters allowed per machine, then the Cloudflare, Firebase and PHP examples and the sync, in parallel.
