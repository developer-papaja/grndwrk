How should an answer be taken back or changed?

Options:
- Undo for some seconds after answering (Cmd+Z or a button in the message), and later Change answer on your own entries in the thread
- Only Change answer: it replaces your entry, and the question is asked again in the Inbox if emptied
- A new answer added after the old one, which wins; the old one stays, crossed out
