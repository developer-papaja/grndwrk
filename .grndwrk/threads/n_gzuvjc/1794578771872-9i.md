The plan, from your answer:

- `/sitemap` looks for trees of screens that share no parent and no link. When it finds more than one, it asks in the thread, naming them: "It looks like you have a user facing part (Home, Shop, Checkout) and an admin facing part (Dashboard, Orders) that are not connected. Show them as two sitemaps?"
- On yes, the parts go into project.md as `parts:` (each a name and its top screens), so the split is decided once and everyone on the project sees it. On no, it never asks again for those trees.
- The Sitemap view gets a Part menu by the title, next to Variant: each part, or All. Remembered per project in the browser, as the variant is.
- Nothing is built yet.
