A step marked No gets a comment field of its own, with pictures pasted or dropped. Doesn't work opens one for the whole (Anything else). Everything you write is kept in this browser per thought until it is sent, so opening another thought or reloading loses nothing. What goes back is built when you send: each step still marked No with its comment, then the rest; a step set back to Works drops out. Not committed yet.

To test:
1. Open a thought that is Ready to test, mark two steps No: each gets its own field. Write in both.
2. Open another thought, then come back: what you wrote is still there.
3. Set one of the two steps back to Works: its field goes, and it is not in what is sent.
4. Write something under Anything else and press Send back to the agent: the entry lists the step that still fails with its comment, then your other words.
