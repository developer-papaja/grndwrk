Like Notion: the top bar has the part picker on the left and icons on the right for Filter, Search and View settings. Filters are a row of pills under the bar, + Filter adds one from a list you can search (New comments, Added by, Waiting for, Type, Topic, Level), Reset takes them away. View settings is a panel of rows (Layout, Columns, Group cards by), each a page of its own, Layout as tiles. Committed in a0e5d95.

To test:
1. Click the filter icon: a row with Status: Open and + Filter.
2. + Filter, type 'add', pick Added by and a person: only their cards, in the board and in the list. Each choice shows a count.
3. Add Type and Level too, then open a pill to change it, and Remove filter on another. Reset takes all away.
4. Set Status to Done and reload: kept. Other filters are gone after a reload.
5. Open View settings (sliders icon): Layout shows Board and Outline as tiles; Columns and Group cards by each open a page with a back arrow, and the panel stays open while you choose.
6. In light mode, every active pill and menu is readable.
