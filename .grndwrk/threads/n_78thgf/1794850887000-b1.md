Built with your mapping. Four text sizes, defined once as tokens in `ui/src/style.css`: `text-tag` 10 px (with 14 px lines), `text-body` 12 px for labels and most text (16), `text-title` 14 px (20) and `text-heading` 16 px (24). Every size in the map moved to one of them: 9 to 10.5 became 10; 11 to 13 became 12; 14 and 15 became 14; 17 and 20 became 16. CLAUDE.md now says so, and that spacing keeps to multiples of four with 6 and 10 allowed (Tailwind's spacing already does). The Comment button on the product's pages keeps its own sizes.

To test:
1. Restart grndwrk and open the map.
2. Look over the board, the left panel, the details panel, the Inbox and Status: text reads at a few even sizes, a little smaller in menus and panels than before.
3. Look for anything that now looks cramped or cut off, above all titles that were 15 px and headings that were 20 px.
