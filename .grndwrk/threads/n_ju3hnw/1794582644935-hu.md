Holding the Comment button down, right-clicking it (or the bar or the tab), or the dots on the bar opens a menu. At the top, where comments go: connected to grndwrk, allow this site in the map, or grndwrk not answering; on a staging site, comments go to your team, waiting to be allowed (with the digits), or not joined yet. Then how many comments wait and how many fixes are ready to test. Then Test N fixes, Comment on this page, Save the waiting comments as a file, on a staging site Join with an invite, Show the digits or Leave, where the button sits (floating, a bar at the bottom, a small tab), and Hide in this tab (Alt+C brings it back).

Testing now works on a staging site too, through the relay: the browser reads its own comments' status as the phone does, a fix ready to test opens on its page with the element marked and the steps, and Approve or Reject goes back through the relay ("Tested in the browser" in the thread). Tried locally in the browser, and the relay part in a test (overlay/feedback.js, overlay/relay.js, src/server/feedback.ts). Not committed yet.

To test:
1. Restart grndwrk once, so the button is the new one.
2. On your product's page, hold the Comment button down (or right-click it): the menu says Connected to grndwrk and lists what it can do.
3. With a fix ready to test for a comment made on this site, the menu says so and Test 1 fix opens it.
4. Pick Bar at the bottom, then Small tab, then Float: the button moves each time.
5. Hide in this tab: the button goes; Alt+C brings it back.
6. On the staging site (after grndwrk relay deploy), make a comment, have it built, then Test it from the menu and reject it: the thread says Tested in the browser with your note.
