Typing in a thought's notes or title felt slow too. On a copy of the same big map, each save (0.4 seconds after the last key, often mid-sentence) froze the page twice for 0.56 seconds: the saved thought came back twice, from the save and from the server's file event, and each time every count of every screen's thoughts went through all the thoughts again (`aboutScreen`, `thoughtsOn`, for the part picker and the Sitemap boxes).

Now each screen's thoughts and counts are worked out in one pass and kept until a thought or screen changes, a thought that comes back unchanged is not taken in again, and a field saves after 1.2 seconds without typing (leaving the field, or the tab, saves at once). Typing with the short pauses a person makes no longer freezes; one pause of 0.25 seconds comes after you stop. The reply box ("Write on the Mac") does not freeze at all.
