Built, not committed or released yet: `clients/android/feedback` (the package, about 5,300 lines of Kotlin) and `clients/android/sample` (an app with a Compose screen and a Views screen), the README in `clients/android`, and `jitpack.yml`. grndwrk's server keeps Android pictures in `shots/android/` and accepts the emulator's name for the Mac, 10.0.2.2. Tried in the emulator against a test map: connecting, a picture of the screen, picking, a comment with its picture, testing a fix, pairing with the code, the menu and the list. The relay, the store and Speak are built but not tried against live services or a microphone.

To test:
1. In `clients/android`, run `./gradlew :sample:installDebug` with grndwrk running in a project on port 7777 (or add `-PgrndwrkServer=http://10.0.2.2:<port>`), and open Vet Clinic in the emulator.
2. The grndwrk button shows with a green dot, and a small camera blinks once: the Sitemap's "not placed yet" has a picture of Appointments.
3. Tap grndwrk, tap a card, try Bigger, then Comment, write a line and Send: it lands on the Feedback page with the picture.
4. Long press grndwrk, Show comments: the comment is under Sent to grndwrk.
5. Open Settings in the app and comment on a switch: the Views screen works the same way.
6. On a real phone on the same Wi-Fi, with `grndwrk --lan`: the phone finds the Mac, asks to be allowed with four digits, and comments arrive.
