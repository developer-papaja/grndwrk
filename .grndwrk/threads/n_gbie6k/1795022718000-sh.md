Two more from testing in an app.

The button went under dialogs and bottom sheets: each is a window of its own above the activity, and the button and its panels were attached to the activity's window. Picking and pictures read the screen behind the sheet too. Now the package finds the app's windows in front (Android 10 and later), and while one is open the button, its tab, the test card and the pick layer sit on it, in the same place on the screen; picking reads what is in front; a comment's picture draws the sheet over the screen, with the text fields in both drawn empty (no picture when one cannot be read); screen pictures wait until it closes. grndwrk's own sheets never count. The sample's Book a visit now opens a bottom sheet to try it on.

Speak said only "not available right now" for most failures: the offline recognizer failed and only two language errors fell back to the usual one. Now it tries three ways (offline, the usual one preferring offline, then online) before giving up, and then says why and what to do: no internet for the language, the language not on the phone, something else listening, the speech service off or busy. Android's error number goes to the log.

The package and the sample build, and the unit tests pass. Not yet tried on a device or in the emulator.

To test:
1. Open a bottom sheet or dialog in your app (the sample's Book a visit): the grndwrk button stays above it and works.
2. Tap grndwrk and pick something in the sheet: the frame lands on it, and the comment's picture shows the sheet with its text field drawn empty.
3. Close the sheet: the button is back where it was, and still works.
4. Tap Speak in the comment sheet: it fills the field. If it cannot, the message says why and what to do.
