The picture of a screen now sits below its text, as wide as the box; boxes are taller when any screen in view has a picture. You can drag the drawing with the mouse to move around, anywhere, also on a box; a click without moving still opens a screen. Not committed yet.

To test:
1. Open the Sitemap, drawn, in a project with pictures of its screens: each picture is below the title and purpose, filling the rest of the box.
2. Zoom out below 80 %: the title stays on top and the picture below it.
3. Press and drag anywhere in the drawing: it moves with the mouse, and the cursor is a grabbing hand.
4. Let go over a screen after dragging: it does not open. Click a screen without moving: it opens.
