Released in 0.4.2, after an independent review of the change. It found no way for comment text to put a mark on a step, and three things I fixed before the release: a step ending in a very long run of spaces made reading the mark slow, a label in brackets such as "Tap [Agent]" was read as a mark, and the web button accepted kinds it does not know. A mark now counts only exactly as the rules write it, in lower case with a colon: `[automated: ...]`, `[agent: ...]`, `[outdated: ...]`.

Your note that a step can be legacy is the third mark, `[outdated: why]`, with the clock icon.
