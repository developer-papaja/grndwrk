Updated the plan with your answers. What changed with them:

- **Anyone on the team fetches**, so every teammate's grndwrk needs the keys that open the colleagues' comments. The plan now has a team key: the first person sets up the relay, teammates connect their grndwrk once with a team link, and the phones' keys are kept at the relay sealed with it. Whoever's grndwrk runs first takes a comment; git brings it to the others.
- **Who wrote it:** each invite carries the colleague's name, and their comments say `from: <name>`, on the card and in the Feedback page.
- **Both ways to join:** paste the link, or scan a QR code. Scanning needs camera access declared in the app; without it, the app only offers the link.
- **Cloudflare Worker**, and **a Beta build configuration** with the README showing how.
