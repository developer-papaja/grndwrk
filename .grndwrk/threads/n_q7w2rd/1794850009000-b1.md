Built. The button's menu on the running product has a part for the screen in view, between where comments go and the actions: This screen, its title and purpose, then For and each persona in the screen's "Who it is for", with their summary and Jobs. On the web the page's path is matched to the routes in the screen's `code:` or `open:`, parameters included (/visits/42 is /visits/{visit}). On the phone the long press adds the same text under the connection once grndwrk answers, by the screen's title, `.grndwrkScreen` name or view type. Not through the relay yet (staging sites, phones away from the Wi-Fi): nothing shows there. A page that is no screen in the map shows nothing extra.

To test:
1. Restart grndwrk with `--app`, so it runs with this, and open your product through it.
2. Go to a page the sitemap has, whose screen has a purpose and "Who it is for" with a persona that has Jobs.
3. Right-click the Comment button (or hold it down): This screen shows the screen's title and purpose, then each persona with summary and jobs. The menu is wider when it does.
4. Go to a page with a parameter in its route (a detail page): the right screen shows.
5. Go to a page that is no screen in the map: the menu is as before.
6. On the phone, after updating the package in the app: long press the grndwrk button on a screen the sitemap knows. The text under the connection gains This screen, its purpose and the personas a moment after the menu opens.
