Who can send comments from the staging site?

Options:
- Only colleagues invited one by one: each opens their invite link once in that browser, and the map allows them with four digits, as for a phone
- Anyone who can open the staging site, with one shared link for the whole team, allowed once
- Anyone on the staging site, no invite: the relay takes every comment and the map shows them to approve
