Thoughts like "We should be able to help ideate" or "Going forward with ideas should feel easy" can never be closed, so they never leave the map. I want to move them to something called guiding principles (övergripande principer). They would not live on the map in the same way, since they cannot be closed, but they should really be in the project, since they matter in everything we do.

Today such a thought stays open for good: it counts as open in every filter, in Status and in `/readiness`, and sits at the top of its column as if something were left to do.

Candidates in this map, all open, under the topic purpose: n_wncw (help ideate), n_adhc (going forward should feel easy), n_vypj (intuitive to work with), n_jtdo (help people). Perhaps also n_rlee (who it is for) and n_sd94 (what success is), which say what grndwrk is rather than how to build it.

A first sketch, not decided (see the thread for what the answers decided):

- **Moving a thought there** takes it off the board and out of the open counts, like Done does, but it is neither done nor parked. In the file: `status: principle`, or a folder `principles/` of its own (one file each, like personas).
- **Where they show:** a list of their own, always in reach: at the top of Status, in the Documents view next to CLAUDE.md, or a strip on the map that can be folded.
- **Where they steer:** the map's rules tell the agent to read them before building and to say when a change goes against one; `/brief` writes them into the starter CLAUDE.md; `/readiness` reads them for Purpose and for the design philosophy foundation; `/challenge` can ask when a thought seems to go against one.
## Decided (2026-11-16)

A strip above the board on the map, folded to one line and opened to read; nothing new in the rail. The agent reads the principles before building and says when a change goes against one, `/brief` writes them into the starter CLAUDE.md, and `/challenge` asks when a thought seems to go against one. A principle can change and go back to the map, but behind a menu, never on a click, with `changed:` written. In the file: `status: principle`. First to move: n_wncw, n_adhc, n_vypj, n_jtdo.
