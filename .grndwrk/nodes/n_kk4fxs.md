Maybe its own thing, apart from keeping the map in a database. Questions can already be marked as waiting for someone (n_rsi4qt), and then you sit down with that person. It would be better to send them a link with the questions that are for them, which they answer wherever they are, without grndwrk, the repo or the map.

Seems possible with what the relay already does (n_0fe7zi): invites by name with a link and a QR code, a browser that joins with a key of its own and waits to be allowed in the map, sealed envelopes both ways. The new part is the other direction: grndwrk leaves the questions for that person at the relay, sealed for their invite, a page the relay serves shows them with their options, and the answers come back as envelopes that grndwrk writes as `**You**` entries (from that person) quoting each question. The map stays local and in git.

To work out: names are kept out of the map unless `personal_info: yes`, so who a question waits for must still reach the link; what the person sees of the thought around the question (only the question, or the whole thought), since the link leaves the team's machines; and how long a link lasts.
