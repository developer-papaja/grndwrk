I would like to read what a screen is for, and who it is for (its personas), when looking at different screens in grndwrk. It would help testing, and understanding feedback: knowing what you are looking at on a given page, and whether a comment or a fix fits what the screen is meant to do and for whom.

Today the purpose and "Who it is for" show only in the Sitemap's screen panel, and the purpose in the part picker when working on one part. Places they could show:

- **The panel of a comment or a thought** that has a screen: the screen's purpose and its personas, each persona opening on a click.
- **Testing a fix** in the panel: the same, above the steps.
- **The running product:** the web button's test card and comment card, and the phone's, from the screen the comment was made on (grndwrk already matches a page to its screen to file comments).
- **Columns by screen** on the board: the purpose under the screen's name.
